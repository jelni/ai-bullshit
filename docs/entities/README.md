# Entities

The primary entities in the game include:

- **Snake:** (`src/snake.rs`) Managed as a sequence of `Point`s. Handles logic for movement, growth, and collision with itself.
- **Bosses:** Adversaries that introduce complex pathing requirements to evade.
- **Lasers/Mines:** Static and dynamic obstacles.
- **Meteors:** Environmental hazards that spawn over time (frequently in Asteroids and Meteor Shower modes). A meteor enters at the top of a column and falls one cell every 2 ticks, smashing obstacles/mines and killing any snake it touches; scheduled Meteor Shower strikes always pick a column no snake occupies or is about to enter. Shower meteors in Asteroids mode can also drift sideways (`Meteor::vx`) and bounce off the side walls; the autopilot predicts their exact trajectory (`meteor_position_after` / `meteor_would_hit`). In Asteroids mode, spawns also avoid every column a snake's body occupies, and each meteor telegraphs its impact cell (`meteor_impact_cells`) before crashing into a crater.
- **LandslideRocks:** Rolling environmental hazards of Landslide mode. A rock spawns at a random edge on a fairness-checked row — never a row any snake occupies or is about to enter, and never a row that already has a rock — guaranteed every 6th tick plus a rare ambient rumble, and then rolls one cell per tick in its direction, smashing obstacles/mines and killing any snake it touches, until it leaves the board. The autopilot predicts the exact roll (`rock_position_after` / `rock_would_hit`).
- **Comets:** Streaking environmental hazards of Comet mode (`Comet { position, dx, dy }`). A comet launches from a random board edge on a fairness-checked diagonal — never a path crossing any snake body or the cell any snake is about to enter — guaranteed every `COMET_PERIOD` (18) ticks plus a rare ambient streak, capped at `MAX_COMETS` (2) in flight. It flies one cell per tick diagonally until it leaves the board, igniting every cell it touches into a burning trail (`fire_tiles` + `fire_ttl`, mirrored into `obstacles`, lethal, burns out after `COMET_TAIL_TICKS`), smashing obstacles/mines and killing any snake it touches. The autopilot predicts the exact diagonal (`comet_position_after` / `comet_would_hit`).
- **Lava/Vents:** In Volcano mode, vents seed a spreading lava field (`lava_tiles`, mirrored into `obstacles`) that is lethal on entry and treated as a hard obstacle by the autopilot.
- **Black hole:** A hazard that spawns and despawns randomly over time, pulls loose items (food, bonus/poison food, power-ups) towards itself, devours adjacent obstacles/mines, and kills any snake whose head enters its cell. In Gravity Well mode the hole is permanent, tugs items every tick, and roams on a scheduled fair surge every 6th tick.
- **Food/Crops/Artifacts:** Items the snake collects to progress.
- **Sand drifts:** In Sandstorm mode, accumulating drifts (`sand_tiles`, mirrored into `obstacles`) that are lethal on entry and treated as hard obstacles by the autopilot.
- **Blaze sources:** In Firestorm mode, permanent blaze sources (`blaze_sources`) seed a spreading flame field (`blaze_tiles: HashMap<Point, u32>` with remaining burn ticks, mirrored into `obstacles`) that is lethal on entry, chars through obstacles, burns out after `BLAZE_BURN_TICKS` ticks, and is treated as a hard obstacle by the autopilot.
- **Spike vents:** In Spike Pit mode, permanent spike vents (`spike_vents`, rendered as a dark-red `V`) erupt telegraphed spikes (`spike_warnings` cracks, then `spike_tiles: HashMap<Point, u32>` with remaining up ticks, mirrored into `obstacles`) on a plus-shaped area every 6th tick; spikes are lethal on entry, retract after `SPIKE_UP_TICKS` ticks (and all at once at tick 200 of each 250-tick cycle), and are treated as hard obstacles by the autopilot.
- **Geyser vents:** In Geyser mode, permanent vents (`geyser_vents`) erupt steam jets every 6th tick: a transient jet field (`geyser_jets: HashMap<Point, u32>` with remaining ticks, mirrored into `obstacles`) that is lethal on entry, never lands on a snake/entity/the cell a snake is about to enter, dissipates after `GEYSER_JET_TICKS` ticks, rests at tick 200 of every 250-tick cycle, and is treated as a hard obstacle by the autopilot.
- **Mirage food:** In Mirage mode, illusory food (`mirage_foods: HashMap<Point, u32>` with remaining ticks) spawns every 6th tick on a fair cell (never on a snake, an entity, an obstacle, or the cell a snake is about to enter), shatters harmlessly when a snake's head walks onto it (no food, no growth), fades after `MIRAGE_TICKS` ticks, clears at tick 200 of every 250-tick cycle, is dispelled by eating real food, and is ignored by the autopilot, which only targets real food.
- **Acid puddles:** In Acid Rain mode, accumulating puddles (`acid_puddles`, mirrored into `obstacles`) that are lethal on entry and treated as hard obstacles by the autopilot.
- **Black hole:** A lethal singularity cell (`black_hole`, rendered as a dark `O`) that spawns occasionally at random and constantly drags loose items (food, bonus/poison food, power-ups) towards itself while swallowing adjacent obstacles and mines. In Event Horizon mode the hole is permanent and additionally drifts one cell every 6th tick and surges every 12th tick (see docs/mechanics).
- **Portals / Warp:** a portal pair (`portals`) that randomly appears in most modes; moving into one portal teleports the head to the other, and the A* pathfinding resolves portals (`travel_dist`) so the autopilot can use them. In Warp mode the pair is permanent, seeded on fair cells at reset, and relocates to a telegraphed fair target pair every 45 ticks.
- **Phase gates:** In Phase Shift mode, two permanent gate walls (`phase_gates`) that toggle every `PHASE_PERIOD` (12) ticks between solid (mirrored into `obstacles`, lethal on entry) and ghost (passable). A gate never solidifies on a snake, on the cell a snake is about to enter, or on any entity, and the autopilot treats gates as permanent walls in `is_safe_final_p` and the trap-avoidance flood fill.
- **Cracked floor:** In Crumble mode, walkable cracked tiles (`crumble_tiles`, rendered as a dark-yellow `:`) that collapse into permanent pits (`obstacles`, lethal on entry) the moment a snake's head leaves them — each crack is a one-time bridge. New cracks appear every `CRUMBLE_PERIOD` (8) ticks on fair cells (never on a snake, the cell a snake is about to enter, an entity, or a hazard), a crack never collapses while another snake is about to enter its cell, all remaining cracks fill in at tick 200 of each 250-tick cycle, eating food reinforces adjacent cracks, and the autopilot treats every crack as a permanent wall in `is_safe_final_p` and the trap-avoidance flood fill.
- **Magnet poles:** In Magnet mode, permanent magnetic poles (`magnet_poles`, `true` = attract / `false` = repel, rendered as cyan `+` / red `-`) that pulse every `MAGNET_PULSE_PERIOD` (6) ticks, shoving every snake one cell toward or away from its nearest pole. A pulse never lands on a snake body, on the cell another snake is about to enter, on any entity or hazard (including the meteors' predicted next cells), so it can never directly kill; the poles relocate to fresh fair cells every `MAGNET_RELOCATE_PERIOD` (24) ticks and eating food reverses the nearest pole's polarity.
- **Wind / gale:** In Gale mode, a board-wide wind (`gale_dir`, rendered as a compass arrow in the top-right corner) that shoves every snake one extra cell along the wind after each move, shifting to a new direction every `GALE_PERIOD` (18) ticks. A gust only lands on a fair, unclaimed cell (never a snake, a hazard, a meteor lane, or another head), so it can never push anyone into a kill; eating food braces the snake against the wind for `GALE_BRACE_TICKS` ticks.
- **Black Hole mode singularity:** In Black Hole mode, the singularity (`black_hole`) that kills any snake whose head enters its cell and drags loose items towards itself. It can spawn ambiently, and in Black Hole mode (`GameMode::BlackHole`) it is permanent: it wanders one cell every 6th tick (never onto a snake, an entity, or the cell a snake is about to enter), devours adjacent obstacles/mines every tick, and devours everything within reach when the snake eats. Treated as a hard hazard by the autopilot in the A* search and the verified-safe path checks.
- **Quicksand:** In Quicksand mode, spreading walkable bog patches (`quicksand`) that are never claimed on a snake, an entity, the cell a snake is about to enter, or a hazard tile. A head standing in the bog sinks one tick at a time (`quicksand_sink`/`quicksand_sink2`/`quicksand_bot_sinks`) and is swallowed after `QUICKSAND_SINK_TICKS` ticks; the whole bog drains at tick 200 of every 250-tick cycle, eating food stomps adjacent bog cells, and the autopilot treats every bog cell as a hard-blocked obstacle.
