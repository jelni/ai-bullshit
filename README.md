# Snake Game

Welcome to our terminal-based **Snake Game**! It’s simple to explain and easy to understand: you control a snake, eat food to grow, avoid obstacles, and survive as long as possible.

## 🚀 Ambitious Vision
We envision a future where this terminal Snake game evolves into the ultimate e-sports platform. We are building the foundational engine for a massively multiplayer, AI-assisted universe where billions of players compete globally in real-time. This is not just a game; it is the genesis of a new digital ecosystem.

## ❄️ Blizzard Mode
We have added a **Blizzard Mode**! A permanent snowstorm (`Weather::Snow`) sweeps the board: every few ticks a snow drift falls on a random empty cell and blocks movement like an obstacle (never on the snake, on items, or on the cell a snake is about to enter). Every 250-tick cycle the snow thaws away, and eating food melts the snow around it. The autopilot handles the storm natively since snow is a hard obstacle in the A* pathfinding and safety verification. Select "Blizzard Mode" from the main menu.

## 🎨 Turf War Mode
We have added a **Turf War Mode**! Every snake paints the cells its body covers, and when the clock runs out the participant with the most painted territory wins. The mode's arena bots are driven by the full verified-safe autopilot and actively expand into unpainted (or enemy-held) frontier tiles via `turf_war_frontier_targets`, so they contest turf instead of beelining into walls.

## ⚡ Storm Mode
We have added a **Storm Mode**! A permanent thunderstorm (`Weather::Storm`) rages over the board: every 6th tick a lightning column devastates a whole row (killing snakes standing in it, damaging bosses, and burning away obstacles in that column). For fairness, a strike never lands on the column a snake currently occupies or is about to enter, so the storm is dangerous but never cheap. Eating food "grounds" the storm, instantly clearing the active lightning column. The autopilot already treats the lightning column as a hard hazard in the A* search and the verified-safe path checks, so the bot rides out the storm natively. Strikes are counted in the status bar (`Strikes: <count>`) and persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Storm Mode" from the main menu.

## 🌋 Quake Mode
We have added a **Quake Mode**! A permanent earthquake (`Weather::Earthquake`) shakes the board: every 6th tick a guaranteed quake either drops a new rubble pile on a random fair cell or shakes an existing obstacle loose. For fairness, rubble never falls on a snake, on any entity, or on the cell a snake is about to enter, and new rubble is capped at 25% of the board. Eating food "stabilizes the ground", instantly clearing one rubble pile. The autopilot handles the quake natively since obstacles are hard constraints in the A* pathfinding and the verified-safe path checks. Quakes are counted in the status bar (`Quakes: <count>`) and persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Quake Mode" from the main menu.

## 🌋 Volcano Mode
We have added a **Volcano Mode**! Two volcano vents (`V`) are seeded on the board at the start of the match, and every 6th tick a scheduled eruption spreads molten lava (`~`) from the lava frontier onto neighboring empty cells (capped at 15% of the board). Lava is lethal: it is mirrored into `obstacles`, so entering a lava tile kills the snake, and the autopilot already treats it as a hard obstacle in the A* search and the verified-safe path checks, so the bot routes around the flow natively. For fairness, lava never spawns on a snake, on any entity, or on the cell a snake is about to enter this tick. At tick 200 of every 250-tick cycle the lava field cools away (the vents remain), and eating food cools the lava adjacent to the eaten cell. Eruptions are counted in `volcano_eruptions` (shown as `Lava: <count> | Eruptions: <count>` in the status bar), and lava/vents are persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Volcano Mode" from the main menu.

## ☄️ Asteroids Mode
We have added **Asteroids Mode**! Meteors rain down on the board 15x more often than usual, and every 6th tick a guaranteed meteor shower drops 2 meteors at once. For fairness, a meteor never spawns on the column a snake currently occupies, the column its head is about to enter, or a column that already has a meteor in flight. Eating food "deflects" the most imminent meteor (the lowest one in flight), giving you a panic button. The autopilot already treats falling meteors as hard hazards in the A* search and the verified-safe path checks, so the bot rides out the bombardment natively. Showers are counted in the status bar (`Meteors: <count>`) and persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Asteroids Mode" from the main menu.
## 🏆 Tournament Mode
We have added a **Tournament Mode**! Selecting "Tournament" from the main menu seeds an 8-entrant single-elimination bracket (`Tournament` in `src/game/tournament.rs`): you plus 7 named bot entrants, each with an ELO rating near yours, shuffled into the bracket. The matches play out live in the arena — both entrants are driven by the full verified-safe autopilot (including Hunter AI cut-offs), so every match is a real bot-vs-bot duel. When a match ends, the winner advances through the quarterfinals, semifinals, and final while both entrants' ELO ratings are updated with the standard K=32 formula (`elo_ratings`); a double-kill is ruled a draw and the match is replayed. The status bar shows the current round and pairing (`Trophy: Quarterfinal 2/4: Viper (1231) vs You (1200)`), match results are announced in the chat log, and the arena resets between matches. When the final is decided, the champion is crowned (`Tournament Champion: <name>!`), `tournaments_played`/`tournaments_won` are recorded in your stats (unlocking the **Tournament Champion** achievement if you take the cup), your `player_elo` is synced to your entrant's final rating, and because your entrant is bot-driven the run counts as `[BOT]` for high-score purposes. The whole bracket is persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Covered by `tests/test_tournament_mode.rs`.

## 🏆 Daily Challenge
We have introduced a **Daily Challenge** mode! Every day, a globally synchronized seed is generated based on the days since the UNIX epoch. This guarantees that all players worldwide face the exact same procedural maze, obstacles, and food spawns for that day, fostering true competitive e-sports. The RNG architecture is fully refactored to use `StdRng` for testability and determinism.

* We added **Quake Mode** (`GameMode::Quake`, menu entry 51): a permanent earthquake that forces `Weather::Earthquake` every tick and triggers a guaranteed quake on every 6th tick (in addition to the ambient earthquake chance) — the quake either drops a new rubble obstacle on a random fair empty cell (capped at 25% of the board) or shakes an existing obstacle loose. Fairness: rubble never falls on a snake's body, on any entity (food, power-ups, portals, mines, bosses, ...), or on the cell a snake is about to enter, and eating food stabilizes the ground by clearing one rubble pile. Quakes are counted in `quake_count` (shown as `Quakes: <count>` in the status bar and persisted in `SaveState`/`HistoryState`), and the autopilot already treats obstacles as hard constraints in A* and verified-safe path selection, so the bot rides out the quakes natively. Covered by `tests/test_quake_mode.rs` and documented in `docs/mechanics/README.md`.

* We added **Volcano Mode** (`GameMode::Volcano`, menu entry 52): two volcano vents spawn at reset and every 6th tick a scheduled eruption spreads lava from the lava frontier onto neighboring empty cells (capped at 15% of the board). Lava is mirrored into `obstacles`/`lava_tiles`, so it kills on entry and is a hard hazard for the A* autopilot, verified-safe path checks, save/load and time-rewind; lava never spawns on snakes, entities, or the cell a snake is about to enter, the lava field cools away at tick 200 of every 250-tick cycle (vents remain), and eating food cools adjacent lava. Covered by `tests/test_volcano_mode.rs` and documented in `docs/mechanics/README.md`.
* We turned **Asteroids Mode** (`GameMode::Asteroids`, menu entry 47) into a full meteor-bombardment mode: on top of the 15x ambient spawn rate, every 6th tick a scheduled shower (`manage_asteroids`) drops 2 meteors on fair columns chosen by `pick_asteroid_columns` — never the column a snake currently occupies, the column its head is about to enter, or a column with a meteor already in flight (ambient spawns in this mode use the same fair picker). Eating food deflects the most imminent meteor (the lowest one in flight) with a "Deflected!" flash. Showers are counted in the new `asteroid_strikes` counter, shown as `Meteors: <count>` in the status bar and persisted in `SaveState`/`HistoryState` (save/load and time-rewind stay consistent); the autopilot already treats falling meteors as hard hazards in A* and `is_safe_final_p`. Covered by `tests/test_asteroids_mode.rs` and documented in `docs/mechanics/README.md`.

* We implemented **Tournament Mode** (`GameMode::Tournament`, menu entry 15) for real: an 8-entrant single-elimination bracket (you + 7 ELO-rated bot entrants, seeded shuffle) played out live in the arena. Both entrants of every match are driven by the verified-safe autopilot with Hunter AI (`calculate_autopilot_move`/`calculate_p2_autopilot_move`), match results update entrant ELO via `elo_ratings` (K=32, draws replay the match), and `handle_tournament_match_end` advances the bracket, resets the arena between matches, and crowns a champion — updating `tournaments_played`/`tournaments_won` stats, the new `TournamentChampion` achievement, and the player's `player_elo`. The bracket lives in `Game::tournament` and is persisted in `SaveState`/`HistoryState`; the status bar shows the live pairing via `Tournament::status_line`. Covered by `tests/test_tournament_mode.rs` and documented in `docs/mechanics/README.md` and `docs/architecture/README.md`.
* We brought the **Turf War arena bots under the full verified-safe autopilot**: `GameMode::TurfWar` now drives its 3 arena bots through the same pipeline as the other arena modes — every candidate route is verified step-by-step (`path_is_safe` with rival-head prediction and predicted enemy occupancy), trap-checked (`path_keeps_tail_reachable`), scored for survivable space, with tail-chase and flood-fill fallbacks — so bots no longer drive straight into walls and die. On top of that, Turf War bots get territory-aware targets: `turf_war_frontier_targets` prepends the nearest cells they have not painted yet (including enemy-owned tiles, which can be reclaimed) ahead of food/power-ups, so each bot actively expands and contests turf instead of only chasing food. Covered by `tests/test_turf_war_autopilot.rs` and documented in `docs/mechanics/README.md`.
* We added **Storm Mode** (`GameMode::Storm`, menu entry 50): a permanent thunderstorm that forces `Weather::Storm` every tick and calls down a guaranteed lightning strike on every 6th tick (in addition to the ambient storm chance), devastating a whole row — killing snakes, damaging bosses, and burning away that column's obstacles. A strike never targets the column a snake currently occupies or is about to enter, and eating food grounds the active strike. Strikes are counted in `storm_strikes` (shown as `Strikes: <count>` in the status bar and persisted in `SaveState`/`HistoryState`), and the autopilot already treats the active column as a hard hazard in A* and verified-safe path selection. Covered by `tests/test_storm_mode.rs` and documented in `docs/mechanics/README.md`.
* We made the autopilot **contested-target aware**: `contested_targets` predicts, for every objective target, whether a rival snake (Player 2 or an arena bot) will reach it strictly first — by wrap/portal-aware travel distance (`travel_dist`) or via the rival's committed head path — and `astar_search_verified_contested` then only races for such a target when no uncontested target yields a verified-safe path, so the bot stops chasing food a rival already wins. Hunt targets on a rival's head (Zombie mode) are never contested, and the filter is inert in single-player or while invincible. This applies to the player bot, the Player 2 bot, and the arena bots. Covered by `tests/test_bot_contested_targets.rs` and documented in `docs/architecture/README.md` and `docs/mechanics/README.md`.
* We added a **Hunter AI** layer to the autopilot: when the bot is strictly longer than a nearby rival snake (in PlayerVsBot, BotVsBot, MassiveMultiplayer, Tron, King of the Hill, Capture the Flag, and Zombie modes), it projects each rival's committed head path up to `Game::CUTOFF_HORIZON` (12) steps ahead (`cutoff_targets`) and searches for a path that lands on a rival's predicted head cell one step before the rival arrives there, so the rival crashes into the bot's body. Candidate kill paths go through the exact same safety pipeline as normal paths (`astar_search` + `path_is_safe` + `path_keeps_tail_reachable`) and are additionally confirmed by `path_cuts_off_rival`, which simulates following the path against every shorter rival's predicted head path using the engine's pre-move-body collision rule; the shortest verified kill wins. A bot carrying the CTF flag never hunts. This applies to the player bot, the Player 2 bot, and the arena bots in MassiveMultiplayer, Tron, and King of the Hill (Zombie arena bots already hunt the player). Covered by `tests/test_bot_cutoff_hunting.rs`.
* We added **Blizzard Mode** (`GameMode::Blizzard`, menu entry 49): a permanent snowstorm in which snow drifts accumulate on the board as hard obstacles (capped at 10% of the board), thaw away every 250-tick cycle, and melt around eaten food. Snow is mirrored into `obstacles`/`snow_tiles` so the A* autopilot, path verification, trap avoidance, save/load and time-rewind all treat it consistently; snow never spawns on snakes, entities, or the cell a snake is about to enter, and the bot is exempt from the usual Snow movement delay in this mode. Covered by `tests/test_blizzard_mode.rs` and documented in `docs/mechanics/README.md`.

* We significantly improved bot pathfinding in `astar_search` by updating the heuristic penalty for lasers to match `get_boss_path`. This ensures bots now strongly avoid lasers as effectively as bosses do.
* We ensured bosses now dynamically avoid turrets by adding a proximity penalty to the A* heuristic in `get_boss_path`. This prevents them from pathfinding directly into a turret's line of fire.
* We significantly improved bot pathfinding in `astar_search` by updating the edge cost penalty for turrets. This ensures bots now strongly avoid turrets.
* We ensured bosses and bots now dynamically avoid mines by heavily increasing the heuristic penalty for exact mine locations and locations close to mines in both `get_boss_path` and `astar_search`. This prevents them from pathfinding directly into mines.
* We overhauled bot and boss pathfinding logic to strongly avoid environmental hazards like Black Holes, Lightning Columns, and Meteors. We did this by heavily increasing the heuristic penalties for locations exactly on or closely surrounding these dynamic threats in both `get_boss_path` and `astar_search`.
* We unified advanced pathfinding capabilities by integrating dynamic edge costs for environmental hazards (Lasers, Mines, Turrets, Black Holes, Lightning Columns, Meteors) into `astar_search`. This bridges the gap between bot and boss navigation, ensuring bots factor in actual traversal risk, matching the complexity of `get_boss_path`.
* We further unified advanced pathfinding capabilities by integrating dynamic edge costs for Lightning Columns and Meteors into `get_boss_path`. This ensures bosses factor in actual traversal risk for all environmental hazards, perfectly matching the complexity of `astar_search`.
* We added a tail-chase survival fallback to the autopilot: when `astar_search` finds no safe path to any target, the bot now paths to its own tail (which always vacates in time) to stay alive until a route to a target opens up, instead of blindly wandering via the flood-fill fallback. This applies to the player bot, the Player 2 bot, and the arena bots in MassiveMultiplayer, Zombie, Tron, and King of the Hill modes.
* We added verified-safe path selection to the autopilot: every A* path is now simulated step-by-step via `path_is_safe`, which re-checks `is_safe_final_p` at each cell's true arrival step (so dynamic threats like lasers and the snake's own still-occupied body are hard constraints, not just soft heuristic penalties). If a path to one target fails verification, the bot retries the remaining targets before falling back to tail-chasing. This applies to the player bot, the Player 2 bot, and the arena bots in MassiveMultiplayer, Zombie, Tron, and King of the Hill modes.
* We made the autopilot trap-aware: `astar_search` now simulates following a candidate path and flood-fills from the arrival cell to verify the snake's tail remains reachable (walls, hazards, other snakes, and its own body except the tail are treated as blocked). Paths that would seal the snake into a dead end are rejected, so the bot keeps searching for a non-trapping route and otherwise falls back to tail-chasing, instead of walking into a lethal pocket. This applies to the player bot, the Player 2 bot, and the arena bots.
* We made the autopilot space-aware: `astar_search_verified` now evaluates every target's own verified-safe, non-trapping path and keeps the most survivable one, scored by the reachable free space after simulating arrival (`reachable_space_after_path`) minus the path length, so equally roomy targets still prefer the shorter route. Instead of always taking the first verified path to the nearest target, the bot now proactively routes toward roomy areas and only commits to a cramped target when nothing better verifies. This applies to the player bot, the Player 2 bot, and the arena bots in MassiveMultiplayer, Zombie, Tron, and King of the Hill modes.
* We made the autopilot predict rival snakes beyond the immediate step: during verified-safe path selection, each rival's head is predicted up to `RIVAL_HEAD_HORIZON` (8) steps ahead along its current direction (through portals and map wrapping), and any path cell that a rival's predicted head occupies at that cell's true arrival step is treated as a head-on collision and rejected. The A* search itself also avoids these predicted lanes so it routes around them instead of only rejecting the path afterwards, and the prediction is skipped while invincible. This applies to the player bot, the Player 2 bot, and the arena bots in MassiveMultiplayer, Zombie, Tron, and King of the Hill modes.
* We brought the flow-field autopilot up to parity with the A* autopilot: arena bots in MassiveMultiplayer and Zombie modes that follow the flow field now extract the implied route via `follow_flow_field` and verify it before moving — every cell must pass `path_is_safe` at its true arrival step, and `path_keeps_tail_reachable` must confirm the bot's tail stays reachable, so flow-field routes can no longer lead bots into lethal pockets. Unsafe or trapping flow-field routes now fall back to the verified A* search, then tail-chasing, then the flood-fill survival move.
* We extended that prediction to full enemy occupancy along committed moves: `path_is_safe` now simulates every other snake (Player 2 and the arena bots) forward along its committed direction queue for up to `ENEMY_PREDICTION_HORIZON` (24) steps and rejects any path step whose cell is predicted to be occupied by an enemy head *or body* at that exact step, so the bot also avoids walking into the lane an enemy's body is about to sweep through, including turns the enemy has already queued. This applies to the player bot, the Player 2 bot, and the arena bots in MassiveMultiplayer, Zombie, Tron, and King of the Hill modes.
