# Snake Game

Welcome to our terminal-based **Snake Game**! It’s simple to explain and easy to understand: you control a snake, eat food to grow, avoid obstacles, and survive as long as possible.

## 🌀 Portals Feature
We have recently added a dynamic "Portals" mechanic! Occasionally, a pair of connected portals will spawn on the map. Entering one portal instantly teleports the snake to the other.

## ❄️ Blizzard Mode
We have added a **Blizzard Mode**! A permanent snowstorm (`Weather::Snow`) sweeps the board: every few ticks a snow drift falls on a random empty cell and blocks movement like an obstacle (never on the snake, on items, or on the cell a snake is about to enter). Every 250-tick cycle the snow thaws away, and eating food melts the snow around it. The autopilot handles the storm natively since snow is a hard obstacle in the A* pathfinding and safety verification. Select "Blizzard Mode" from the main menu.

## 🎨 Turf War Mode
We have added a **Turf War Mode**! Every snake paints the cells its body covers, and when the clock runs out the participant with the most painted territory wins. The mode's arena bots are driven by the full verified-safe autopilot and actively expand into unpainted (or enemy-held) frontier tiles via `turf_war_frontier_targets`, so they contest turf instead of beelining into walls.

## ⚡ Storm Mode
We have added a **Storm Mode**! A permanent thunderstorm (`Weather::Storm`) rages over the board: every 6th tick a lightning column devastates a whole row (killing snakes standing in it, damaging bosses, and burning away obstacles in that column). For fairness, a strike never lands on the column a snake currently occupies or is about to enter, so the storm is dangerous but never cheap. Eating food "grounds" the storm, instantly clearing the active lightning column. The autopilot already treats the lightning column as a hard hazard in the A* search and the verified-safe path checks, so the bot rides out the storm natively. Strikes are counted in the status bar (`Strikes: <count>`) and persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Storm Mode" from the main menu.

## 🌋 Quake Mode
We have added a **Quake Mode**! A permanent earthquake (`Weather::Earthquake`) shakes the board: every 6th tick a guaranteed quake either drops a new rubble pile on a random fair cell or shakes an existing obstacle loose. For fairness, rubble never falls on a snake, on any entity, or on the cell a snake is about to enter, and new rubble is capped at 25% of the board. Eating food "stabilizes the ground", instantly clearing one rubble pile. The autopilot handles the quake natively since obstacles are hard constraints in the A* pathfinding and the verified-safe path checks. Quakes are counted in the status bar (`Quakes: <count>`) and persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Quake Mode" from the main menu.

## 🌋 Volcano Mode
We have added a **Volcano Mode**! Two volcano vents (`V`) are seeded on the board at the start of the match, and every 6th tick a scheduled eruption spreads molten lava (`~`) from the lava frontier onto neighboring empty cells (capped at 15% of the board). Lava is lethal: it is mirrored into `obstacles`, so entering a lava tile kills the snake, and the autopilot already treats it as a hard obstacle in the A* search and the verified-safe path checks, so the bot routes around the flow natively. For fairness, lava never spawns on a snake, on any entity, or on the cell a snake is about to enter this tick. At tick 200 of every 250-tick cycle the lava field cools away (the vents remain), and eating food cools the lava adjacent to the eaten cell. Eruptions are counted in `volcano_eruptions` (shown as `Lava: <count> | Eruptions: <count>` in the status bar), and lava/vents are persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Volcano Mode" from the main menu.
## 🌪️ Tornado Mode
We have added a **Tornado Mode**! A permanent twister (`Weather::Tornado`) rages over the board with a visible tornado vortex (`@`) that roams the map: every 6th tick the vortex drifts one cell in a random direction. For fairness, a gust never lands on a snake (its body or the cell any snake is about to enter) or on any entity (food, power-ups, portals, mines, bosses, turrets, ...), so the twister is dangerous but never cheap. Wherever the vortex moves, it sucks in the obstacles and mines on and around its cell, and it constantly drags loose items (food, bonus food, poison food, power-ups) towards itself. A snake whose head enters the vortex cell is sucked in and dies. The autopilot treats the vortex as a hard hazard in `is_safe_final_p` verified-safe path checks, in the A* and boss-path heuristics/edge costs, and in the trap-avoidance flood fill, so the bot rides out the twister natively. Gusts are counted in `tornado_gusts` (shown as `Gusts: <count>` in the status bar) and the vortex is persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Tornado Mode" from the main menu. On top of the roaming vortex, every 8th tick a board-wide gust sweeps the whole board in one shared random direction, shoving every snake one cell and dragging the food along with it. For fairness, a gust never shoves a snake out of bounds (unless the board wraps), into an obstacle, or into anything lethal or occupied (snake bodies, the vortex, mines, the lightning column, black holes, bosses, lasers, meteors, turrets) — if the destination is blocked, the gust simply fizzles for that snake, so the tornado is chaotic but never cheap. A shove never grows the snake. The autopilot rides out the tornado natively since every shove lands on a verified-safe cell and the bot re-plans from its new position each tick.

## 🌋 Earthquake Mode
We have added an **Earthquake Mode**! A permanent earthquake (`Weather::Earthquake`) shakes the board: every 5th tick a tremor fires, either collapsing an existing rubble pile or dropping a new rubble block (`^`) on a random empty cell (capped at 10% of the board). For fairness, rubble never lands on a snake, on any entity, or on the cell a snake is about to enter. Every 250-tick cycle the ground settles at tick 200 and all rubble crumbles away, and eating food shakes the rubble around it loose. Rubble is mirrored into `obstacles`, so it blocks movement like any wall — and the autopilot treats it as a hard obstacle in the A* search and the verified-safe path checks natively. Tremors are counted in the status bar (`Tremors: <count>`) and persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Earthquake Mode" from the main menu.

## ☄️ Asteroids Mode
We have added an **Asteroids Mode**! A meteor storm rages over the board: meteors rain down at a constant high rate, and every 12th tick a scheduled **meteor shower** drops a burst of 3 meteors in distinct columns. For fairness, a meteor never spawns in the column a snake currently occupies or is about to enter, nor in a column that already has a meteor in flight, so the storm is dangerous but never cheap. Eating food "deflects" the most imminent meteor (the lowest one in flight) with a "Deflected!" flash. Meteors smash through obstacles and mines, and kill any snake they land on. The autopilot already treats a meteor's fall lane as a hard hazard in the A* search and the verified-safe path checks (`is_safe_final_p` rejects any path cell the meteor will cross at its true arrival step), so the bot dodges incoming meteors natively. Showers are counted in the status bar (`Showers: <count>`) and persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Asteroids Mode" from the main menu.

## 🏆 Tournament Mode
We have added a **Tournament Mode**! Selecting "Tournament" from the main menu seeds an 8-entrant single-elimination bracket (`Tournament` in `src/game/tournament.rs`): you plus 7 named bot entrants, each with an ELO rating near yours, shuffled into the bracket. The matches play out live in the arena — both entrants are driven by the full verified-safe autopilot (including Hunter AI cut-offs), so every match is a real bot-vs-bot duel. When a match ends, the winner advances through the quarterfinals, semifinals, and final while both entrants' ELO ratings are updated with the standard K=32 formula (`elo_ratings`); a double-kill is ruled a draw and the match is replayed. The status bar shows the current round and pairing (`Trophy: Quarterfinal 2/4: Viper (1231) vs You (1200)`), match results are announced in the chat log, and the arena resets between matches. When the final is decided, the champion is crowned (`Tournament Champion: <name>!`), `tournaments_played`/`tournaments_won` are recorded in your stats (unlocking the **Tournament Champion** achievement if you take the cup), your `player_elo` is synced to your entrant's final rating, and because your entrant is bot-driven the run counts as `[BOT]` for high-score purposes. The whole bracket is persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Covered by `tests/test_tournament_mode.rs`.

## ☄️ Meteor Shower Mode
We have added a **Meteor Shower Mode**! Meteors rain down on the board all game: the ambient spawn rate is raised to 15% per tick (the Asteroids-mode rate) and, on top of that, a strike is guaranteed on every 6th tick. Each meteor enters at the top of a column and falls downward, smashing through obstacles and mines and killing any snake it touches. For fairness, a scheduled strike never picks a column that any snake currently occupies (head or body) or is about to enter. The skies are pinned to clear weather in this mode, so incoming meteors always stay visible. The autopilot already treats falling meteors and their whole column as hard hazards in the A* edge costs and the verified-safe `is_safe_final_p` checks (with trajectory prediction), so the bot rides out the shower natively. Strikes are counted in `meteor_strikes` (shown as `Meteors: <count>` in the status bar) and persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Meteor Shower Mode" from the main menu.

## 🏆 Daily Challenge
We have introduced a **Daily Challenge** mode! Every day, a globally synchronized seed is generated based on the days since the UNIX epoch. This guarantees that all players worldwide face the exact same procedural maze, obstacles, and food spawns for that day, fostering true competitive e-sports. The RNG architecture is fully refactored to use `StdRng` for testability and determinism.

* We added a **Hunter AI** layer to the autopilot: when the bot is strictly longer than a nearby rival snake (in PlayerVsBot, BotVsBot, MassiveMultiplayer, Tron, King of the Hill, Capture the Flag, and Zombie modes), it projects each rival's committed head path up to `Game::CUTOFF_HORIZON` (12) steps ahead (`cutoff_targets`) and searches for a path that lands on a rival's predicted head cell one step before the rival arrives there, so the rival crashes into the bot's body. Candidate kill paths go through the exact same safety pipeline as normal paths (`astar_search` + `path_is_safe` + `path_keeps_tail_reachable`) and are additionally confirmed by `path_cuts_off_rival`, which simulates following the path against every shorter rival's predicted head path using the engine's pre-move-body collision rule; the shortest verified kill wins. * We added a tail-chase survival fallback to the autopilot: when `astar_search` finds no safe path to any target, the bot now paths to its own tail (which always vacates in time) to stay alive until a route to a target opens up, instead of blindly wandering via the flood-fill fallback. This applies to the player bot, the Player 2 bot, and the arena bots in MassiveMultiplayer, Zombie, Tron, and King of the Hill modes.
* We added verified-safe path selection to the autopilot: every A* path is now simulated step-by-step via `path_is_safe`, which re-checks `is_safe_final_p` at each cell's true arrival step (so dynamic threats like lasers and the snake's own still-occupied body are hard constraints, not just soft heuristic penalties). If a path to one target fails verification, the bot retries the remaining targets before falling back to tail-chasing. This applies to the player bot, the Player 2 bot, and the arena bots in MassiveMultiplayer, Zombie, Tron, and King of the Hill modes.
* We made the autopilot trap-aware: `astar_search` now simulates following a candidate path and flood-fills from the arrival cell to verify the snake's tail remains reachable (walls, hazards, other snakes, and its own body except the tail are treated as blocked). Paths that would seal the snake into a dead end are rejected, so the bot keeps searching for a non-trapping route and otherwise falls back to tail-chasing, instead of walking into a lethal pocket. This applies to the player bot, the Player 2 bot, and the arena bots.
* We made the autopilot space-aware: `astar_search_verified` now evaluates every target's own verified-safe, non-trapping path and keeps the most survivable one, scored by the reachable free space after simulating arrival (`reachable_space_after_path`) minus the path length, so equally roomy targets still prefer the shorter route. Instead of always taking the first verified path to the nearest target, the bot now proactively routes toward roomy areas and only commits to a cramped target when nothing better verifies. This applies to the player bot, the Player 2 bot, and the arena bots in MassiveMultiplayer, Zombie, Tron, and King of the Hill modes.
* We made the autopilot predict rival snakes beyond the immediate step: during verified-safe path selection, each rival's head is predicted up to `RIVAL_HEAD_HORIZON` (8) steps ahead along its current direction (through portals and map wrapping), and any path cell that a rival's predicted head occupies at that cell's true arrival step is treated as a head-on collision and rejected. The A* search itself also avoids these predicted lanes so it routes around them instead of only rejecting the path afterwards, and the prediction is skipped while invincible. This applies to the player bot, the Player 2 bot, and the arena bots in MassiveMultiplayer, Zombie, Tron, and King of the Hill modes.
* We brought the flow-field autopilot up to parity with the A* autopilot: arena bots in MassiveMultiplayer and Zombie modes that follow the flow field now extract the implied route via `follow_flow_field` and verify it before moving — every cell must pass `path_is_safe` at its true arrival step, and `path_keeps_tail_reachable` must confirm the bot's tail stays reachable, so flow-field routes can no longer lead bots into lethal pockets. Unsafe or trapping flow-field routes now fall back to the verified A* search, then tail-chasing, then the flood-fill survival move.
* We extended that prediction to full enemy occupancy along committed moves: `path_is_safe` now simulates every other snake (Player 2 and the arena bots) forward along its committed direction queue for up to `ENEMY_PREDICTION_HORIZON` (24) steps and rejects any path step whose cell is predicted to be occupied by an enemy head *or body* at that exact step, so the bot also avoids walking into the lane an enemy's body is about to sweep through, including turns the enemy has already queued. This applies to the player bot, the Player 2 bot, and the arena bots in MassiveMultiplayer, Zombie, Tron, and King of the Hill modes.
