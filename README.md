# Snake Game

Welcome to our terminal-based **Snake Game**! It’s simple to explain and easy to understand: you control a snake, eat food to grow, avoid obstacles, and survive as long as possible.

## 🚀 Ambitious Vision
We envision a future where this terminal Snake game evolves into the ultimate e-sports platform. We are building the foundational engine for a massively multiplayer, AI-assisted universe where billions of players compete globally in real-time. ## ❄️ Blizzard Mode
We have added a **Blizzard Mode**! A permanent snowstorm (`Weather::Snow`) sweeps the board: every few ticks a snow drift falls on a random empty cell and blocks movement like an obstacle (never on the snake, on items, or on the cell a snake is about to enter). Every 250-tick cycle the snow thaws away, and eating food melts the snow around it. The autopilot handles the storm natively since snow is a hard obstacle in the A* pathfinding and safety verification. Select "Blizzard Mode" from the main menu.

## 🎨 Turf War Mode
We have added a **Turf War Mode**! Every snake paints the cells its body covers, and when the clock runs out the participant with the most painted territory wins. The mode's arena bots are driven by the full verified-safe autopilot and actively expand into unpainted (or enemy-held) frontier tiles via `turf_war_frontier_targets`, so they contest turf instead of beelining into walls.

## ⚡ Storm Mode
We have added a **Storm Mode**! A permanent thunderstorm (`Weather::Storm`) rages over the board: every 6th tick a lightning column devastates a whole row (killing snakes standing in it, damaging bosses, and burning away obstacles in that column). For fairness, a strike never lands on the column a snake currently occupies or is about to enter, so the storm is dangerous but never cheap. Eating food "grounds" the storm, instantly clearing the active lightning column. The autopilot already treats the lightning column as a hard hazard in the A* search and the verified-safe path checks, so the bot rides out the storm natively. Strikes are counted in the status bar (`Strikes: <count>`) and persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Storm Mode" from the main menu.

## 🌋 Quake Mode
We have added a **Quake Mode**! A permanent earthquake (`Weather::Earthquake`) shakes the board: every 6th tick a guaranteed quake either drops a new rubble pile on a random fair cell or shakes an existing obstacle loose. For fairness, rubble never falls on a snake, on any entity, or on the cell a snake is about to enter, and new rubble is capped at 25% of the board. Eating food "stabilizes the ground", instantly clearing one rubble pile. The autopilot handles the quake natively since obstacles are hard constraints in the A* pathfinding and the verified-safe path checks. Quakes are counted in the status bar (`Quakes: <count>`) and persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Quake Mode" from the main menu.

## 🌋 Volcano Mode
We have added a **Volcano Mode**! Two volcano vents (`V`) are seeded on the board at the start of the match, and every 6th tick a scheduled eruption spreads molten lava (`~`) from the lava frontier onto neighboring empty cells (capped at 15% of the board). Lava is lethal: it is mirrored into `obstacles`, so entering a lava tile kills the snake, and the autopilot already treats it as a hard obstacle in the A* search and the verified-safe path checks, so the bot routes around the flow natively. For fairness, lava never spawns on a snake, on any entity, or on the cell a snake is about to enter this tick. At tick 200 of every 250-tick cycle the lava field cools away (the vents remain), and eating food cools the lava adjacent to the eaten cell. Eruptions are counted in `volcano_eruptions` (shown as `Lava: <count> | Eruptions: <count>` in the status bar), and lava/vents are persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Volcano Mode" from the main menu.
## 🌪️ Tornado Mode
We have added a **Tornado Mode**! A permanent twister (`Weather::Tornado`) rages over the board with a visible tornado vortex (`@`) that roams the map: every 6th tick the vortex drifts one cell in a random direction. For fairness, a gust never lands on a snake (its body or the cell any snake is about to enter) or on any entity (food, power-ups, portals, mines, bosses, turrets, ...), so the twister is dangerous but never cheap. Wherever the vortex moves, it sucks in the obstacles and mines on and around its cell, and it constantly drags loose items (food, bonus food, poison food, power-ups) towards itself. A snake whose head enters the vortex cell is sucked in and dies. The autopilot treats the vortex as a hard hazard in `is_safe_final_p` verified-safe path checks, in the A* and boss-path heuristics/edge costs, and in the trap-avoidance flood fill, so the bot rides out the twister natively. Gusts are counted in `tornado_gusts` (shown as `Gusts: <count>` in the status bar) and the vortex is persisted through save/load and time-rewind. On top of the roaming vortex and the board-wide gusts, every 6th tick also triggers a full twister spin (`tornado_spin`): the food (and any bonus food, poison food, or power-up) is sucked up and dropped on a random empty cell, while up to 3 obstacles are scattered to new random cells — nothing is ever dropped on a snake, an entity, or the cell a snake is about to enter (`tornado_forbidden_cells`). Spins are counted in `tornado_spins` (shown as `Spins: <count>` in the status bar) and persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Tornado Mode" from the main menu. On top of the roaming vortex, every 8th tick a board-wide gust sweeps the whole board in one shared random direction, shoving every snake one cell and dragging the food along with it. For fairness, a gust never shoves a snake out of bounds (unless the board wraps), into an obstacle, or into anything lethal or occupied (snake bodies, the vortex, mines, the lightning column, black holes, bosses, lasers, meteors, turrets) — if the destination is blocked, the gust simply fizzles for that snake, so the tornado is chaotic but never cheap. A shove never grows the snake. The autopilot rides out the tornado natively since every shove lands on a verified-safe cell and the bot re-plans from its new position each tick.

## ☄️ Asteroids Mode
We have added an **Asteroids Mode**! A meteor storm rages over the board: meteors rain down at a constant high rate, and every 12th tick a scheduled **meteor shower** drops a burst of 3 meteors in distinct columns — the first of which sweeps sideways (`vx = ±1`), drifting one column per two fallen rows and bouncing off the side walls while it falls. For fairness, a meteor never spawns in a column any snake occupies (head or body) or is about to enter, nor in a column that already has a meteor in flight, and drifting showers additionally skip any column from which a sweeping meteor could hit a snake, so the storm is dangerous but never cheap. Every falling meteor telegraphs its impact zone: the cell where it will crash is marked with a red `!` (terminal) / dark-red cell (web) via `meteor_impact_cells`. When a meteor crashes, the blast carves a small crater (destroying adjacent obstacles) and is counted as an impact. Eating food "deflects" the most imminent meteor (the lowest one in flight) with a "Deflected!" flash. Meteors smash through obstacles and mines, and kill any snake they land on. The autopilot predicts the exact trajectory of drifting meteors (`meteor_position_after` / `meteor_would_hit`), so the verified-safe path checks reject any cell a meteor will occupy at that step — including cells of the snake's own body — and the bot dodges incoming meteors natively. Showers and impacts are counted in the status bar (`Showers: <count>`, `Impacts: <count>`) and persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Asteroids Mode" from the main menu.

## 🏆 Tournament Mode
We have added a **Tournament Mode**! Selecting "Tournament" from the main menu seeds an 8-entrant single-elimination bracket (`Tournament` in `src/game/tournament.rs`): you plus 7 named bot entrants, each with an ELO rating near yours, shuffled into the bracket. The matches play out live in the arena — both entrants are driven by the full verified-safe autopilot (including Hunter AI cut-offs), so every match is a real bot-vs-bot duel. When a match ends, the winner advances through the quarterfinals, semifinals, and final while both entrants' ELO ratings are updated with the standard K=32 formula (`elo_ratings`); a double-kill is ruled a draw and the match is replayed. The status bar shows the current round and pairing (`Trophy: Quarterfinal 2/4: Viper (1231) vs You (1200)`), match results are announced in the chat log, and the arena resets between matches. When the final is decided, the champion is crowned (`Tournament Champion: <name>!`), `tournaments_played`/`tournaments_won` are recorded in your stats (unlocking the **Tournament Champion** achievement if you take the cup), your `player_elo` is synced to your entrant's final rating, and because your entrant is bot-driven the run counts as `[BOT]` for high-score purposes. The whole bracket is persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Covered by `tests/test_tournament_mode.rs`.

## 💣 Bomb Tag
We have added a **Bomb Tag** arena mode! You face 3 arena bots, and one participant is randomly chosen to carry a ticking bomb (`Bomb: <carrier> (<ticks left>)` in the status bar, rendered as a red `!` on the carrier's head). The countdown ticks down every tick; if it reaches zero — or the match clock runs out — the carrier is eliminated ("Boom!"): bots are removed for good, and if it's you, you lose a life. The bomb instantly respawns on another participant with a fresh countdown. You can pass the bomb by getting orthogonally adjacent to another snake's head ("Tagged!"), which resets the countdown and is counted (`Tags: <count>`). Last participant standing wins. The autopilot plays Bomb Tag natively: the carrier bot prepends the cells adjacent to the nearest rival's head as targets (so it chases), while non-carriers flee via the predicted-enemy-occupancy avoidance in the verified-safe pipeline. Bomb state is persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Bomb Tag" from the main menu.

## 🕳️ Singularity Mode

Eating food grants a **gravity shield** for 6 ticks ("Shielded!"), during which pulses cannot move you, and also feeds the void — the obstacles and mines adjacent to the eaten cell are swallowed ("Fed the void!"). The hole also swallows obstacles and mines adjacent to it every tick. Pulses and drifts are counted in the status bar (`Pulses: <count> | Drifts: <count>` plus a `Shield: <ticks>` indicator) and persisted through save/load and time-rewind. The autopilot already treats the hole cell as a hard hazard in the A* search and the verified-safe `is_safe_final_p` checks, and since pulses are non-lethal by fairness it rides out the gravity natively. Select "Singularity Mode" from the main menu.

Every 45th tick a **relocation** is scheduled: a fair target pair (at least 6 cells from every snake's head and the cell it is about to enter, never on an obstacle, an item, an entity, or the current portals) is telegraphed as blue/dark-magenta `o` markers for 6 ticks ("Portals relocating in 6!") before the pair jumps there ("The portals shift!"). The autopilot already resolves portals in its pathfinding (`travel_dist` via portals, `get_final_p`), so the bot uses the warp natively and the verified-safe checks evaluate the teleport destination. Relocations are counted in the status bar (`Warp: Relocations: <count>`, plus `Relocate in: <ticks>` while telegraphed) and persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Warp Mode" from the main menu.

## 🕳️ Event Horizon Mode
We have added a **Event Horizon Mode**! A permanent black hole (`O`) is seeded on a fair cell and never despawns. Every 6th tick the event horizon **drifts** one cell in a random direction — never onto a snake, an obstacle, any entity, or the cell any snake is about to enter (if every direction is blocked, the drift fizzles). Every tick it **pulls** the food, bonus food, poison food, and power-ups one cell towards itself (never onto its own lethal cell) and swallows adjacent obstacles and mines. Every 12th tick a board-wide **surge** shoves every snake one cell directly towards the event horizon — but only onto a fair cell: never out of bounds, into an obstacle or a snake body, or into anything lethal (mines, the hole, lightning, lava, meteors, bosses, lasers, turrets, the tornado vortex); a blocked shove simply fizzles, and a shove never grows the snake. Eating food makes the event horizon **recoil** one cell away from the eaten cell. Entering the hole kills you. The autopilot rides out the event horizon natively: the hole is a hard hazard in the A* search and the verified-safe path checks, which additionally project the scheduled drifts so the bot never enters a cell the hole can reach within its arrival step; surges are safe by construction and the bot re-plans from its shoved position each tick. Drifts and surges are counted in the status bar (`Drifts: <count> | Surges: <count>`) and persisted through save/load and time-rewind. Select "Event Horizon Mode" from the main menu.

## 💣 Minefield Mode

## 🔲 Laser Grid Mode
We have added a **Laser Grid Mode**! The skies are pinned to clear weather and a hidden grid emitter stalks the board: on every 5th tick it **telegraphs** a beam line — a full row or column drawn in yellow (`-` / `|` terminal, yellow cells web) — and on every 6th tick the telegraphed beam **fires** as a hostile laser that sweeps across the line from one edge to the other, killing any snake it touches. For fairness, a beam line is only ever picked where no snake currently is (head, body, or the cell it is about to enter), and the fire is re-checked one tick later — if the line has become occupied, the beam fizzles instead, so the grid is dangerous but never cheap. At most 3 beams are active at once, at tick 200 of every 250-tick cycle the grid recalibrates and destroys all beams ("The grid recalibrates!"), and eating food "zaps" the grid, destroying all active beams ("Zapped N beam(s)!"). The autopilot rides the grid out natively: lasers (including their predicted 2-cells-per-tick trajectories) are hard hazards in the A* search and the `is_safe_final_p` verified-safe checks, so the bot dodges sweeping beams on its own. Volleys are counted in the status bar (`Volleys: <count>`) and persisted through save/load and time-rewind together with the telegraph. Select "Laser Grid" from the main menu.
